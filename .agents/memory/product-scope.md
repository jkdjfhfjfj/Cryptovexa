Customers receive a **“Virtual rental inside the app”**, not access to real remote machines or physical properties. The intended geographic audience is **“All countries”**.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected the virtual-only model when asked what a paid rental should provide, and stated “All countries” when asked where the business will operate.

**How to apply:** Keep customer disclosures and purchase acknowledgements consistent with virtual-only rentals. Do not imply real hardware, property access, or investment returns. Intended worldwide reach is not proof of country-by-country legal or provider eligibility; launch review remains necessary.

## Customer-facing entry points and banner

Do not include an administrator sign-in link on the customer login page. Do not display the site-wide “Virtual rentals only. Fees buy in-app rental time, not access to buildings or computing hardware. No investment returns are provided” banner.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested both removals. The administrator login remains a separate route.

**How to apply:** Keep customer sign-in separate from staff entry points. Preserve the confirmed virtual rental model and the purchase acknowledgement; do not reintroduce the removed site-wide banner.
