Customers receive a **“Virtual rental inside the app”**, not access to real remote machines or physical properties. Each rental locks its required principal and pays the admin-configured, time-based profit at maturity; early stops release principal and pay prorated profit. The intended geographic audience is **“All countries”**.

**Why:** The user selected virtual in-app rentals, then explicitly replaced the earlier fee-only model with principal-locked rentals and maturity/early-stop profit settlement, and stated “All countries” as the intended audience.

**How to apply:** Keep disclosures and purchase acknowledgements consistent with virtual in-app rentals and the configured profit schedule. Do not imply real hardware or property access. Intended worldwide reach is not proof of country-by-country legal or provider eligibility; jurisdiction-specific launch review remains necessary.

## Customer-facing entry points and banner

Do not include an administrator sign-in link on the customer login page. Do not display the site-wide “Virtual rentals only. Fees buy in-app rental time, not access to buildings or computing hardware. No investment returns are provided” banner.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested both removals. The administrator login remains a separate route.

**How to apply:** Keep customer sign-in separate from staff entry points. Preserve the virtual rental model and rental-terms acknowledgement; do not reintroduce the removed site-wide banner.
