---
name: Pooled Binance deposits
description: The operator's accepted shared-address attribution model for deposits.
---

The operator explicitly chose pooled Binance deposit addresses with automatic credits and accepts the risk of users claiming another user's public transaction.

**Why:** the operator preferred pooled addresses and selected automatic credits after the shared-address misattribution risk was disclosed.

**How to apply:** keep strict transaction ID, coin, network, address, memo/tag, amount, and successful-status matching; prevent duplicate transaction IDs; and leave ambiguous or unmatched deposits in manual review. Keep manual deposit mode available as an admin switch.
